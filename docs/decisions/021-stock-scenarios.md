# Stock profit and short-position sizing

Build 0.21.0 adds private, non-posting stock long/short profit and short-position risk scenarios. Existing spot, leveraged and long-risk scenarios remain backward compatible. No stock borrowing, short financial position, margin balance or posted transaction is created.

Stock profit accepts entry/exit share prices, quantity, entry/exit fee amounts and, for shorts, borrowing/dividend payment amounts. Long gross profit is quantity times exit minus entry; short gross profit reverses that price difference. Entered costs reduce net profit. Return is measured against entry share value, not broker margin or available proceeds. Fractional quantities are arithmetic inputs, not a claim of broker borrow availability. Long requests with nonzero short-specific costs are rejected; the form hides and excludes those costs in long mode while retaining them for switching back.

Short risk uses an absolute stop price above entry. It reserves estimated total costs from a positive risk budget before dividing by the adverse price distance and rounding down to whole shares. Costs must be below the budget, and the remaining budget must permit at least one share. Outputs show price loss, costs, total risk and unused budget. Entry notional is exposure, not required margin. Margin remains unavailable. Both short and long risk help explicitly distinguish loss budget from invested capital and explain that actual gaps, slippage and changing costs may increase losses.

All financial arithmetic uses BCMath decimal strings in the WordPress-independent domain. API inputs reject JSON numbers, foreign workspace access, unsupported direction/currency and extra fields. Existing workspace generation guards discard late calculator responses. Changing any calculator input also clears its result and discards a pending response for those older inputs. Output amounts use shared string-based decimal presentation and adjacent alert/status feedback.

Schema remains 8; no SQL migration or database update is required. All eight bundled SQL files remain packaged. Next work is bought call/put premium close-out and expiration scenarios.
