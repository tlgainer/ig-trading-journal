# Manual observations and reproducible reporting

The owner requested manual prices/FX, reports and saved views before returning to UI/UX work. No market-data provider is selected or contacted.

Schema 8 stores append-only observations with effective date, UTC observation time, source, reason, valid-through date and optional superseded observation ID. Prices may explicitly be zero with a documented valuation note; FX must be positive. Prices are tied to workspace asset identity and quote currency. Direct FX means one unit of native currency in the workspace base currency. Owner/manager membership is required to write. Corrections retain identity and effective date and cannot replace an already superseded record. The base currency rate is exactly one by definition, never an inferred foreign rate.

Selection uses the latest effective date on or before the requested date, highest ID on ties, excluding superseded observations. Expired values remain available and labeled stale; missing values are null. New observation records affect future report runs; saved report payloads never change. Historical reports use current corrected financial history and current known observations, rather than pretending to reproduce what a user knew in the past.

Reports reconstruct native cash and FIFO lots through the date in the workspace timezone. Basis conversion uses lot acquisition-date FX, realized proceeds use disposal-date FX, and market value uses valuation-date FX. Fees are already included by the native ledger; they are not added again. Reports retain the exact active ledger inputs, observation records, trade context, filters, calculation version, selection policies and a watermark. They do not change posted financial rows or replay snapshots. Saved report IDs remain scoped to current membership in their workspace.

Activity and gain periods include both endpoints. Holdings ignore the start date and represent the as-of position. Account/asset/class/currency filters define valuation scope; action/strategy/tag/image filters define activity scope. Allocation includes the filtered holdings and account/currency-filtered cash. If any market value/required FX is missing, the denominator and percentages are null. Unknown basis affects basis/gain/return, not a known market value. Lifetime purchase spend remains separate from remaining basis. Economic gain requires an explicit comparable whole-account period, complete non-stale coverage and no starting balance inside that period; otherwise its amount is null with a reason.

Strategy performance groups by captured immutable version and native currency. Only groups marked closed with closure in the period, all fills posted by as-of, at least one buy and sell, and net linked quantity zero enter win-rate calculations. Canonical account FIFO determines realized gain, inclusive of acquisition/disposal fees. Break-even groups remain in the denominator and are not wins. Open, planned, archived, partially posted and unbalanced groups are excluded. Different currencies are never added as native amounts. Journal edits can change a new strategy report's group context; old report snapshots retain the earlier context.

Income cannot be derived correctly until dividend/interest accounting is implemented. The report explicitly says unavailable; an empty income result is not a zero-income assertion. Reconciliation is labeled not reconciled. CSV/portable exports and restore evidence remain separate work.

Personal saved views are writable by any active member with viewing permission, scoped by workspace and actor, with expected-revision checks and append-only revisions. They store filters, not a frozen financial result. Report and observation mutations use workspace locking, idempotency and atomic audit. Synchronous generation is bounded to 1,000 accounts and 10,000 events/assets/observations/trades/fills; larger jobs remain future operational work.
